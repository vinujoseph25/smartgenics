# SmartGenics — Industrial IoT & Automation

> Product engineering case study spanning IIoT, industrial automation, connected operations and healthcare interoperability.

**SmartGenics** is an industrial technology product experience focused on connecting operational environments, devices and software systems to improve visibility, automation and decision-making.

This repository is retained as a **product engineering case study**. It demonstrates how a technically complex technology offering can be translated into a coherent digital product experience.

## Product vision

SmartGenics brings together industrial technology concepts across IoT, automation, monitoring and integration:

```text
Devices / PLCs / Systems
          ↓
     Edge / Gateway
          ↓
   Secure data flow
          ↓
 Cloud / Integration
          ↓
 Monitoring / Analytics
          ↓
 Operational decisions
```

## Engineering perspective

The strongest engineering signal in this repository is the **translation of a complex technical offering into an understandable product experience**.

Key considerations include:

1. **Information architecture** — separating industries, products and services so users can navigate a complex offering.
2. **Responsive delivery** — supporting desktop and mobile form factors.
3. **Reusable presentation** — applying consistent navigation, content, cards, typography and responsive patterns.
4. **Technical communication** — presenting industrial and healthcare technology concepts in a structured way.
5. **Extensibility** — keeping product, industry and service areas independently evolvable.
