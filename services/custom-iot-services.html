Custom IOT Services

Every industry is different and has different requirements. We understand industry needs and customize systems and solutions according to customer needs with the power of IoT technology. Industrial IoT bridges the gap between traditional industrial infrastructure and emerging technologies such as edge computing, machine learning, cloud and mobile. IIoT helps industries streamline industrial operations to improve productivity and efficiency. IoT is a disruption of industrial technology, enabling industries to streamline their operations.

We act as a custom IOT application builder based on your requirements for all kinds of sensors such as flow meters, power meters, temperature sensors, pressure sensors, gases and more. Our system is configurable with third-party sensors compatible with the RS485 protocol.

IoT Solution Development

SmartGenics experts can help you develop your IoT project from scratch, adapt an already running solution, or integrate the software into your existing infrastructure. In this way, we ensure that customers receive IoT solutions that improve operational efficiency, accelerate intelligent decision-making, and lead to overall market success.


Implementing Software

As part of IoT software development, we help customers implement solutions we produce. If you have specific detailed requirements for IoT solutions, our development team will realize the project in this way. We pay great attention to the integration of IoT solutions with respective systems and their implementation for a precise synchronous operation.
