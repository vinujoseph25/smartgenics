SCADA Solutions

Process Solutions specializes in the design, construction and integration of custom SCADA systems for a wide range of industries and applications, including water / wastewater, municipalities, food and beverages, and biotechnology. Our engineering team has hundreds of years of experience in implementing PLC-based SCADA solutions, including system planning and analysis, hardware and software configuration, PLC programming, communication network design and integration, and control cabinet manufacturing. We can provide comprehensive project services such as system commissioning. Documentation, troubleshooting, and maintenance.

SCADA Programming Platforms

Design, build and integrate SCADA systems on all industry-leading software platforms, including Rockwell Factory Talk, Siemens WinCC OA, Aveva (Wonderware) Systems Platform and Ignition. Experience with these platforms gives you the opportunity to provide the right recommendations for the right system for your needs and goals. Develop monitoring and control software with well-structured code to facilitate future feature additions and updates. Our software user interface is designed for ease of use, resulting in a clean and consistent interface that helps reduce distractions and focus on important information.
