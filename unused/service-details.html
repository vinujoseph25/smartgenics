Industrial Automation

The First Industrial Revolution dawned with the arrival of steam power, water power, and mechanization. Hundred years post the first revolution came to the Second Industrial Revolution which was a phase of mass production, standardizations, and innovative scientific discoveries. Then came the era of the Third Industrial Revolution, also known as the Digital Revolution, which introduced the concept of automation and digitalization. Half a century passes and we currently bear witness to the Fourth Industrial Revolution, or as many call it Industry 4.0.

Industry 4.0 is inclined more toward automation, particularly smart automation, and focuses on processes within the manufacturing industry, including The Internet of Things (IoT), The Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT), smart factories, cloud computing, and cyber-physical systems (CPS), and cognitive computing. Industrial automation is an integral part of Industry 4.0 and is closely associated with control engineering. Industrial automation ensures the use of control devices, including PC / PLCs / PACs to monitor industrial processes, equipment, and technology by reducing labor intervention wherever possible.

Advantages Of Industrial Automation

Industrial automation has been fundamental in shifting the focus from increased productivity to enhanced quality and flexibility. In today’s competitive landscape, manufacturers face a range of challenges, including harsh manufacturing conditions, meeting standard safety levels, growing competition, and complicated supply chain procedures. All these factors push the industrialists to incorporate industrial automation in their establishments.

  • High Productivity
  • Superior Product quality
  • Reduced Production Cost
  • High Safety
  • Increased Data Security and Accuracy
