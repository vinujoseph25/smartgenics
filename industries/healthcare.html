Healthcare

In each decade, a particular industry becomes more popular and grows faster than others. In the early 1900s, the agricultural industry boomed, employing millions of people around the world. The computer industry boomed in the late 90s and changed lives around the world forever. And, right now, the healthcare industry is booming. In the United States, the healthcare industry is the fastest growing industry. What's even better is that healthcare is integrated with technology to make life simple, efficient and effective for the world community. The combination of healthcare with technology gives rise to a new term known as modern medicine.

What exactly is modern medicine?

Modern medicine mainly uses insights from IoT (Internet of Things) in healthcare to make it more authoritative, available and flexible. Modern medicine has opened up a whole new level of innovative treatment in healthcare. For example, electronic health records (EHRs) are an integral component of human healthcare monitoring systems. As astronomical amounts of data continue to be produced and stored, it becomes increasingly difficult to capture real-time patient tracking information in healthcare archives. Although modern medicine has managed to become as tech-savvy as other companies in the retail, infrastructure and education sectors, it still fails to manage big data and exchanges. data. Fortunately, for thousands of providers, the Rapid Healthcare Interoperability Resource (FHIR®) is beginning to shed light on a better way forward. Let's see how!

How SmartGenics Makes Impact?

As an innovative startup, has a unique vision to revolutionize the healthcare industry through smart technology, including bringing the IoT concept to healthcare. The company has launched a product, called Babble, which has a unique Health Connect module / Babble's Health Connect module has an HL7® FHIR® integration standard that regulates how information is securely exchanged. FHIR® has a framework that can be interpreted by any system and is used in a variety of ways, such as in cloud communications, EHR/EMR platforms, and healthcare facilities.

Why should users and vendors get excited about Babble? Because it has the potential to make healthcare very similar to other internet-based experiences that customers enjoy in other industries. The concept of IoT is gaining popularity in the healthcare industry, but there are few tools that act as a bridge between patient-generated healthcare data and provider workflows. Babble is the link that connects patients and providers. Applications built on the FHIR platform are capable of generating patient-generated health data analytics and providing users with solutions for disease management or patient care.

For example, regular monitoring devices can assess for anomalies, but lack the ability to load this information in real time. FHIR can act as a real-time interactive tool; for example, the HL7® FHIR® standard can be exploited in smart wearable devices. FHIR enables wearable IoT devices to interact with the EHR in real time and provide immediate decision support.
